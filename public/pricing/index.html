What does amzlisting.ai do?

It connects Amazon marketplace and competitor research with customer insights, listing copy, creative briefs, and product visuals. Five AI specialists contribute different perspectives to the same listing workflow.

What is included in Individual?

The Individual plan is $95 per month and includes 15 listings per month, with five-specialist research, competitor and customer insights, SEO copy, high-CTR main image concepts, conversion-optimized listing images, bulk variation creation, and A/B testing creative options.

Who is the Team plan for?

The $495 per month Team plan is designed for agencies and teams working across multiple brands. Team seats and monthly listing allowances will be announced at launch.
